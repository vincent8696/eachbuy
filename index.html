Frequently asked questions

What is your MOQ and can I mix models?

Most models start at 300–1,000 pcs. For container orders you can freely mix models across all nine categories to reach a 20-foot or 40-foot container.

Do you provide OEM / ODM and private label service?

Yes. Logo printing, custom colour and packaging design are handled in-house, and packaging artwork is provided free of charge for confirmed orders.

How fast can you ship?

Standard lead time is 20–35 days after deposit and artwork confirmation. Samples go out in 3–7 working days.
